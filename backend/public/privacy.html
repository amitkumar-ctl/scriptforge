Privacy Policy

Welcome to ScriptForge. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

By using ScriptForge, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1 Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information (via Google OAuth)

When you sign in using Google OAuth, we receive the following from Google:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google profile picture (if available)

We do not receive or store your Google password.

1.2 User-Generated Content

ScriptForge stores the scripts and content you create on our platform in our database. This includes:

  • Scripts, prompts, and generated content
  • Your saved projects

This content is stored to provide you with a continuous, personalized experience across sessions and devices.

1.3 Usage Data

We may collect basic usage data such as features used, session duration, and error logs to improve our service. This data is not linked to your specific content.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your account
  • Store and display your scripts and generated content
  • Improve and maintain the ScriptForge platform
  • Communicate important service updates
  • Respond to support requests
We do not use your scripts or generated content to train AI models. Your content is yours.

3 Third-Party Services

3.1 Anthropic (Claude AI)

AI Provider

ScriptForge uses Anthropic's Claude API to power AI-based script generation. When you generate content, your prompts are sent to Anthropic's API to produce a response.

Anthropic processes this data according to their own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing Anthropic's Privacy Policy for details.

We do not share your name, email, or account information with Anthropic — only the content of your prompts is transmitted.

3.2 Google OAuth

Authentication

We use Google OAuth for authentication only. Google's Privacy Policy governs data you share with Google during sign-in. We do not share your ScriptForge data with Google.

3.3 Hosting & Infrastructure

Infrastructure

ScriptForge is hosted on Railway (railway.app). Your data is stored on their infrastructure and is subject to their security practices.

4 Data Storage & Security

Your account information and scripts are stored in our secure database. We implement industry-standard security practices, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data transmission
  • Restricted access to production databases
  • Regular security reviews

No system is 100% secure. If you suspect unauthorized access to your account, please contact us immediately.

5 Data Retention

We retain your account information and scripts for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information and content within 30 days, unless required by law to retain it.

6 Payments (Coming Soon)

ScriptForge plans to introduce paid features in the future. When payment functionality is added, we will use a trusted third-party payment processor (such as Stripe).

We will never store your raw credit card or banking information on our servers. This Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect payment data practices before any paid features go live.

7 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability — Request an export of your scripts and content

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

8 Children's Privacy

ScriptForge is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you via email or a prominent notice within the app. Your continued use of ScriptForge after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
